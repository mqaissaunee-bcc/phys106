Presentation version

Teach from the modules in class. Each module turns into slides you can step through with a presentation clicker, and the simulations and activities still work on the slides, so you can run them in front of the class.

Students use the regular site, which is the textbook version: the same pages without the Present buttons. Opening this page turns the Present buttons on in this browser, so on the computer you teach from, every module page will show them.

In the classroom

Plug in your clicker and press Present. The forward and back buttons change slides; the blank button (or the B key) blacks out the screen, and pressing it again brings the slide back. Press F for full screen and Esc to end. When you end, the page opens to the section you were last showing.

On guided problems, the clicker reveals the worked solution one step at a time before moving to the next slide. If a slide has more than fits on screen, the clicker scrolls it first. Press O, or click the slide counter, to jump to any slide.

With a projector, choose Presenter view from the control bar and drag the new window to your laptop screen. It shows what comes next, a timer, and definitions for the glossary terms on the current slide. Your clicker works in either window. If nothing opens, allow pop-ups for this site.

In a brightly lit room, the light theme projects best. Use A+ and A− on the control bar to make the text bigger or smaller for the room.
