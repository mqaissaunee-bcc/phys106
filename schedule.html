The course is organized into 13 modules, each built to fill one 2-hour-45-minute class session. In a 15-week semester that is one module per week, with a week for each exam. Shorter terms (such as 7 or 11 weeks) cover more than one module in some weeks; your Canvas course lists which modules each week covers.

This is a tentative schedule; changes may be made during the semester as needed.
