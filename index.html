PHYS 106, 3 credits

Astronomy: the universe, module by module

Thirteen modules from our own sky to the edge of the observable universe. Each module has original readings, hands-on simulations you can run in your browser, activities to do on your own, and a self-check quiz.

Start Module 1 See the schedule

How to use this site

Work through each module before and during class. As you finish a section, mark it complete; your progress shows up here and stays in this browser. Run every simulation and try every activity: they are part of the reading, not extras. Work through the activities, then take the self-check quiz until you are comfortable. Homework is posted in Canvas. Hover over or tap any dotted-underlined term for its definition.

Everything you do here (progress, answers, journal, and notes) is saved only in the browser you are using. Visit My work each week to download a backup, and to move your work to another computer or into Canvas.

Working outside at night? Switch the theme to night (red). Red light protects your dark-adapted vision, the same trick astronomers use at the telescope.
