My work

Your reading progress, activity answers, quiz scores, Moon journal, and notes are saved only in this browser, on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

That keeps your work private, but it also means it stays behind if you switch computers, clear your browser data, or open the course inside Canvas instead of directly. A backup file solves all three: download one each week, keep it somewhere safe such as OneDrive, and restore it wherever you need it.
