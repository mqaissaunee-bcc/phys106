Orders of Magnitude Explorer

Astronomy spans a range of sizes, times, and masses too big to picture. Here every step along the line is ten times the one before, a power of ten, so atoms and the whole observable universe fit on one scale. Choose what to measure, select landmarks, compare two of them, or test your sense of scale in the estimation game.

Remember: moving one tick to the right means 10 times larger; two ticks, 100 times; three ticks, 1,000 times. The scientific notation section of the Math toolkit explains the notation, and the calculator’s "Magnitude line" view places any answer on the same scale.
