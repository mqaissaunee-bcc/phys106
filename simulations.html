Simulations

Free simulations from other sites that go with each module, from PhET Interactive Simulations at the University of Colorado Boulder and the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group. Each one opens in a new tab; "See it in" takes you to the box in the module with suggested things to try.

Every module also has its own built-in simulations, listed in its "Plan your time" table.

Our Place in the Universe

  • Motions of the Sun Simulator (opens in a new tab) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group (HTML5 version hosted by Columbia University)
    Watch the Sun’s daily path across the sky change through the year. See it in Module 1
  • Lunar Phase Simulator (opens in a new tab) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group (HTML5 version hosted by Columbia University)
    See the Moon’s position in its orbit, its phase, and where it appears in the sky at the same time. See it in Module 1

The Science of Astronomy

  • PhET Kepler’s Laws (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Explore all three of Kepler’s laws with orbits you can reshape. See it in Module 2

Motion, Gravity, Light, and Telescopes

  • PhET Gravity Force Lab (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Change two masses and the distance between them, and watch the gravitational force. See it in Module 3
  • PhET Gravity and Orbits (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Choose the Sun, Earth, and Moon, and turn on the gravity force arrows and path. See it in Module 3
  • Hydrogen Atom Simulator (opens in a new tab) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group (HTML5 version hosted by Columbia University)
    Fire photons at a hydrogen atom and watch its electron jump between energy levels. See it in Module 3
  • PhET Blackbody Spectrum (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Change an object’s temperature and watch its thermal spectrum shift. See it in Module 3
  • PhET Geometric Optics (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Bend light with lenses and mirrors to see how telescopes form images. See it in Module 3

The Solar System and Its Origin

  • PhET My Solar System (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Build your own system of stars and planets and watch gravity shape their orbits. See it in Module 4

Worlds Up Close

  • PhET Greenhouse Effect and Molecules and Light (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    See how greenhouse gases trap infrared light, from the whole planet down to single molecules. See it in Module 5
  • Gas Retention Simulator (opens in a new tab) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group (HTML5 version hosted by Columbia University)
    Test which gases a world can hold onto, depending on its temperature and gravity. See it in Module 5

Small Bodies and Other Planetary Systems

  • Exoplanet Transit Simulator (opens in a new tab) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group (HTML5 version hosted by Columbia University)
    Make a planet pass in front of its star and watch the light curve dip. See it in Module 6
  • Circumstellar Habitable Zone Simulator (opens in a new tab) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group (HTML5 version hosted by Columbia University)
    Change a star’s mass and see where its habitable zone lies and how it shifts over time. See it in Module 6

The Building Blocks of the Universe

  • PhET Rutherford Scattering (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Shoot particles at atoms the way Ernest Rutherford did and discover the nucleus. See it in Module 8
  • PhET Quantum Wave Interference (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Send electrons and photons through slits, one at a time, and build up the pattern. See it in Module 8
  • PhET Wave on a String (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Make standing waves on a string with fixed ends, a model of why atoms have only certain energy levels. See it in Module 8

The Sun and Measuring Stars

  • Eclipsing Binary Simulator (opens in a new tab) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group (HTML5 version hosted by Columbia University)
    Watch two stars eclipse each other and see how their light curve reveals their sizes and orbits. See it in Module 9
  • H-R Diagram and Stellar Evolution simulators (opens in a new tab) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group
    This collection includes an H-R Diagram explorer and a Stellar Evolution Simulator that works well with this section and Module 10. Open the page and choose "HR Diagram." See it in Module 9

Star Birth and Stellar Lives

  • PhET Build a Nucleus (opens in a new tab) from PhET Interactive Simulations, University of Colorado Boulder
    Add protons and neutrons to build nuclei and see which combinations are stable. See it in Module 10

Galaxies and Their Evolution

  • Hubble’s Law Simulator (opens in a new tab) from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln astronomy education group
    This collection includes a Hubble’s Law Simulator that shows the expansion of the universe. Open the page and choose "Hubble’s Law Simulator." See it in Module 12
