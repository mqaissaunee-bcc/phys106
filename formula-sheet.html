Distances and light

QuantityFormula or value
1 astronomical unit (AU)150 million km (1.5 × 10⁸ km)
1 light-year9.46 trillion km (9.46 × 10¹² km)
1 parsec3.26 light-years
Speed of light, c300,000 km/s (3 × 10⁸ m/s)
Light-travel timetime = distance ÷ speed of light; light takes 8.3 minutes to cross 1 AU

Orbits and gravity (Modules 2–3)

QuantityFormula or value
Kepler’s third lawp² = a³ (p in years, a in AU): cube a, then take the square root
Mass from an orbitM = a³ ÷ p² (solar masses, AU, years)
Newton’s second lawF = m × a
Weightweight = m × g; g = 9.8 m/s² on Earth
Inverse-square ruledouble the distance: force (or brightness) becomes 1/4 as strong

Solar system and worlds (Modules 4–6)

QuantityFormula or value
Densitydensity = mass ÷ volume
Spheresurface area = 4πr²; volume = (4/3)πr³

Relativity and particles (Modules 7–8)

QuantityFormula or value
